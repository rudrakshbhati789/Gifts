To You!

Meri jaan, shayad main har roz tumse apna pyaar sahi tarah se jata nahi paata, lekin sach yeh hai ki meri duniya tumse shuru hoti hai aur tum par hi khatam. Tumhari ek muskaan meri saari thakaan mita deti hai, aur tumhari ek chhoti si baat mere dil ko bohot sukoon de jaati hai. Jab tum khush hoti ho, meri duniya roshan ho jaati hai, aur jab tum udaas hoti ho, mera dil sirf tumhe theek dekhna chahta hai. Tum sirf meri wife nahi ho, tum meri best friend ho, meri dua ho, aur meri zindagi ka sabse khoobsurat faisla ho. Main zindagi ke har mandir, har raaste par tumhara haath thaam kar chalna chahta hoon. Bas itna kehna tha — main tumse aaj bhi utna hi pyaar karta hoon, aur har kal tumse aur zyada karta rahunga. ❤️
