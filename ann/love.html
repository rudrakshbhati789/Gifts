💌 My Love Letter For You 💖

Meri Jaan,

Tum meri zindagi ka sabse khoobsurat hissa ho.
Har din tumhare saath ek nayi khushi, ek naya ehsaas deta hai.
Tumhara haath pakad kar chalna, tumhari aankhon me pyaar dekhna…
Yeh sab mere liye duniya ki sabse badi khushiyan hain.

Main hamesha tumhare saath rahunga — har muskuraahat, har aansu,
har pal, har saans mein sirf tum ho… aur sirf tum hi rahogi.

I love you forever, meri Jaan ❤️💫
